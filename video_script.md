## Recording Tips

- **Loom settings:** Browser tab + mic only. **No webcam** — keep the focus on the app, not your face.
- **Practice once before recording.** A single dry run will save you ~90 seconds of restarts. Don't memorize — internalize the beats.
- **Pre-load before hitting record:**
  - Streamlit app open in the browser tab you'll share
  - Mock data loaded for Patient #247
  - Demo Control state set to **"fresh"** (Session 4 not yet ended)
  - Closing slide ready in a second tab if you're using one
- **Pace:** Speak slightly slower than feels natural. Loom flattens energy — what feels measured to you reads as confident on playback. Aim for ~150 words/minute, not 180.
- **One take is fine.** A natural delivery with a stumble beats a polished re-take. If you misspeak a stat, correct it in stride — judges trust humans more than robots.
- **Optional Pixverse opener:** Only add if you finish the build with 5+ minutes to spare. Generate a 5-sec clip of a phone showing the anchor card on a kitchen counter — soft morning light. Drop it in front of the Loom by recording it as your first visual. If Pixverse is flaky, skip it. The patient anchor card screenshot works just as well.
- **Mute notifications.** Slack, Mail, Calendar — all off. Nothing kills a one-take like a ping at 1:47.
